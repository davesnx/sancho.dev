- People have "relationships" with tools (they solve a problem you have, you master the tool).
  - You know what are the limits, edges, corner cases of your tool. You "understand" the tool. Then you master it.
- People create "itentities" around the tools they use (you identify with the tool, you believe vim is better than emacs)
- Those tools are deterministic (same input, same output)
- LLMs are undeniably extremly useful: from helping on creating the best expresso shot, to helping me organise the last travel and also help create an interview process for extremly qualified cantidates for my company
- LLMs being used in rare places (if you underatnd llms, you know how wrong they are, and how good they sound)
  - Medical treatments
  - Medial diagnosis
  - Psicology
  - Human behaviour
- LLMs are undeterministic
  - you have your own version of the LLM
- LLMs are also reliable (24/7, on your side)
- LLMs have a human touch
- Over time
