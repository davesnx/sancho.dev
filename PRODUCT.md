# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves technical readers, open-source peers, potential collaborators,
and people who want to understand David's work and interests. They arrive to
read an article, watch a talk, inspect a project, or learn who David is without
working through a conventional portfolio pitch.

## Product Purpose

sancho.dev is David Sancho's personal publishing and identity site. It brings
technical writing, recorded talks, work history, open-source projects, and
personal context into one durable public home. Success means that readers can
find and read the work easily, understand the person behind it, and trust that
the site reflects the same care as the engineering it describes.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, technical, and personal. The voice is direct and thoughtful, with enough
character to feel handmade. It avoids marketing language and does not turn
David's work or life into a sales page.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolios built from repeated cards and technology logos.
- SaaS landing-page cliches, gradients, glass panels, and inflated claims.
- Editorial-magazine styling used as decoration rather than to improve reading.
- Dense terminal cosplay that treats monospace type as a substitute for design.
- Decorative motion or color that competes with long-form content.
- AI-generated visual patterns such as identical card grids, tracked eyebrows,
  numbered section labels, and oversized generic hero statements.

## Design Principles

1. Writing comes first. Layout, color, and interaction make reading easier.
2. Personal facts stay exact. Inspiration can shape tone but cannot invent a
   biography.
3. Minimal means deliberate. Remove what has no job, then give the remaining
   details enough craft to feel specific.
4. Technical depth stays approachable. Articles, talks, work, and system details
   should remain useful without turning the site into documentation software.
5. The site proves its quality. Shared behavior, responsive layouts,
   accessibility, and performance must work on the real pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA text contrast. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
semantic headings and landmarks, accessible names, and reduced-motion
preferences. Keep core content usable without hover, pointer movement, device
orientation, animation, or color alone. Test behavior around the existing
599 px, 899 px, and 1199 px breakpoints.
