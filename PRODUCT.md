# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and peers who read about David Sancho's work, projects, talks, and technical ideas.

## Product Purpose

Present David's work and writing in a direct, useful, and personal format.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, and personal.

## Anti-references

Preserve the current design rather than following a separate visual reference.

## Design Principles

- Keep the writing central.
- Make interactions clear and quiet.
- Preserve the established visual system.
- Prefer direct information over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain the site's current accessibility practices and support keyboard navigation and reduced motion where practical.
